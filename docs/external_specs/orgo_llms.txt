# Orgo

> Orgo is cloud computers for AI agents, with your own agents inside. Every Linux and Windows computer is its own isolated virtual machine with its own disk, a full desktop, a browser, and a terminal, reachable over HTTP and VNC. Linux and Windows are self-serve. A Mac or an iPhone 12 comes from Get one in the app while in stock, and the iPhone 17 is by request. A new computer is online in seconds and runs until you stop it.

Links are relative to `https://docs.orgo.ai`. API base URL: `https://www.orgo.ai/api`.

## Orientation

- Send `Authorization: Bearer $ORGO_API_KEY` on every request. Keys begin with `sk_live_`; [create one](https://www.orgo.ai/start).
- Spec 2.0.0 declares 114 operations in 14 groups, 38 of them GET.
- A computer's `status` is one of `creating`, `running`, `restarting`, `updating`, `suspending`, `suspended`, `resuming`, `frozen`, `stopped`, `error`, `deleted`. `stop` freezes, so a stopped computer reads `frozen`.
- `?screen=` is honoured by 8 actions only: screenshot, click, mouse-move, drag, type, key, scroll, bash. The rest run on the boot screen.
- Plans and per-computer ceilings live at [orgo.ai/#pricing](https://www.orgo.ai/#pricing).
- Also in the spec, without a page of their own: GET /templates/global/{namespace}/{name}, DELETE /templates/{namespace}/{name}/{version}/build, DELETE /templates/run, PATCH /workspaces/{id}, GET /computers/{id}/resize.

## What Orgo is not

- **Not a browser.** A browser tool gives an agent a browser tab. Orgo gives it a full computer.
- **Not an AI agent.** Orgo provides the computer, not the agent. Bring Claude Computer Use, OpenAI's CUA, Hermes Agent, OpenClaw, or your own loop.
- **Not a general cloud platform.** Orgo runs one thing: persistent computers for AI agents. That focus shapes the rest.
